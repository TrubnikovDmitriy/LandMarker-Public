Privacy Policy for Land Marker

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application Land Marker (package name dv.trubnikov.landmarker, the “App”), developed by Dmitriy Trubnikov (“I”, “me”, the “Developer”), handles your information.

Summary

  • The App works completely offline. It does not request the Internet permission and cannot send any data to the Developer or to any server.
  • The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the Developer or any third party.
  • There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Everything the App uses — your location, photos you take, and your progress — is processed and stored only on your device.

What the App does

Land Marker is a location-based app for finding landmarks. It shows reference photos of places and helps you find them using a compass-style map. When you reach a place, you take a photo with your camera, and the App compares it with the reference photo on your device to confirm that you found the right spot.

Data the App accesses and why

Location (precise and approximate)

With your permission, the App uses your device's location to show the distance and direction to landmarks and to check that you are close to a landmark. Location is requested only while the relevant screen of the App is open (the App does not use location in the background). Your live location while searching for landmarks is used in memory only and is not stored as a location history. If you create your own landmark, the coordinates of that landmark are saved locally on your device and will be included if you choose to export and share that landmark set with others.

The App obtains location through the Fused Location Provider of Google Play services, which is part of your device's operating environment. Google's handling of location data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, not by this policy.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the App uses the camera so you can photograph landmarks. Photos are compared with the reference photo using a machine-learning model that runs entirely on your device (TensorFlow Lite). Photos you take are saved in the App's private storage on your device so you can see the landmarks you have found. Photos are stored in the App's private storage on your device and are not accessible to other apps, unless you explicitly choose to export and share a landmark set that you created.

Motion sensors

The App reads the device's orientation sensor to show which direction you are facing (compass). This data is used in real time and is not stored.

App data

The App stores your progress (which landmarks you have found, landmark photos and their coordinates) and completion codes in a local database and in the App's private settings on your device.

Sharing of data

The Developer does not receive any of your data and therefore does not share it with anyone. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

User-initiated sharing (Export & Import): The App allows you to export landmark sets (containing reference photos, their geographic coordinates, and set title and description) as a file and share it with other users using Android's standard system sharing menu (for example, via Quick Share or Bluetooth), as well as import landmark set files created by others. This sharing happens solely at your direction; the Developer has no access to the exported or imported files.

Device backups

Android cloud backups and automatic device-to-device data transfers are disabled for the App, so your photos and progress remain strictly on your device unless you explicitly export a landmark set.

Data retention and deletion

All data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage (Settings → Apps → Land Marker → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the App.

Your choices

You can grant or revoke the location and camera permissions at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Land Marker → Permissions). Without these permissions some features of the App will not work.

Security

Data is kept in the App's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Because the App does not transmit data, there is no data in transit to protect.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change (for example, if online features are added), this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the App's store listing.
